# ATLAS selected — 3 October 2026

The owner selected **ATLAS**, an original eagle, and requested an energetic, quick-witted personality with Kevin-Hart-inspired comic timing and more purposeful animation. ATLAS keeps its own identity, language and voice. The approved elegant light conversation and graphite voice UI remains the reference. Implementation is authorized after the operational follow-ups; current revamp work is preparation only.

## Selected production direction

Use a tapered umber silhouette, pale throat, restrained golden beak and charcoal collar. Express personality through attentive eyes, brow feathers, head angle and compact wing gestures. Combine poised judgment with fast reactions, confident warmth, occasional double-takes and a small satisfied celebration. The neutral/listening face must remain welcoming and mature, including during a serious decision.

Kevin Hart is an inspiration for energy, comic pauses and expressive timing. ATLAS is an original character with original phrasing and performance, without a celebrity likeness, identity or voice. Quiet, Balanced and Expressive remain independent presentation settings; voice output, character visibility and motion each have their own controls.

## Historical round-two exploration

Six named expression-study screens were requested in the existing [Stitch project](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/projects/4399395118624914882), each with relaxed confidence, attentive listening, a comic double-take and a small satisfied celebration.

| Working name | Archetype | Intended personality | Main design risk to assess |
|---|---|---|---|
| Otto | Sloth | Perceptive, reassuring, dry comic timing | A sleepy appearance could imply a slow assistant |
| Bruno | Bear | Warm confidence, grounded competence, precise small gestures | Broad proportions must avoid looking like a plush toy |
| Piper | Sparrow | Quick wit, observant energy, expressive wing gestures | Avoid juvenile eyes and constant frenetic movement |
| ATLAS — selected | Eagle | Poised judgment, sharp observation, warm quick wit and expressive comic timing | Avoid severity, militaristic posture or a judgmental face |
| Ren | Fox | Theatrical charisma, intelligent listening, quick reactions | Maintain originality and warmth rather than a familiar film look or sneaky expression |
| Bao | Panda | Calm self-belief, patient warmth, tiny proud celebration | Familiarity and cuddly proportions may feel less distinctive or mature |

These began as working names and generated concept stills. Selection establishes a creative direction; it does not establish a cleared brand identity, authored model ownership, rig, tested animation or voice. ACE and all unselected studies are historical comparison material.
