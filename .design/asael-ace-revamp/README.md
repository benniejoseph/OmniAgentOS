## Recommended delivery

1. **Complete the operational follow-ups, then lock ATLAS and the shared visual system.** Use the exported and visually inspected eagle concept, resolve tokens and page templates, and prove character consistency, expression/motion and web/native rendering before final asset production.
2. **Ship one complete companion experience.** Shell, conversation, truthful activity, decisions, rich results, personality settings, and immersive voice over the existing governed runtime.
3. **Migrate every existing page family.** Work/results, memory/capture, specialist domains, capabilities/administration, public/access/lifecycle, and native counterparts. Preserve existing operations and deep links.
4. **Add ongoing responsibilities.** Start with one read-only watch with accepted evidence, meaningful-change detection, quiet notifications, cumulative limits, and complete pause/end behavior.
5. **Expand deliberately.** Reviewed effects and one verified external conversation channel follow the read-only pilot. A general cloud computer and always-listening voice remain separate projects.

The first usable milestone is the companion experience over today's capabilities. The whole-site milestone requires every row of the page plan and native parity matrix to pass; the first attractive conversation page alone is not completion.

## Scope and assumptions

- Asael remains a private, owner-focused product with existing tenant/actor governance.
- Web establishes the reviewed visual system first. Native asset feasibility is tested at the start, and shared contracts stay compatible while mobile/macOS presentation follows by family.
- ATLAS is the selected original eagle character. Asael remains the product and assistant name; the appearance does not replace the executing Agent/principal. The existing Supervisor Agent is also named Atlas, so visual selection must not rewrite its charter or other specialist personas.
- Both light and dark themes apply across the product. Voice respects the selected theme, with the approved graphite treatment as its dark reference.
- The owner has authorized the implementation sequence after the operational follow-ups. Current revamp work is preparation only: no runtime flags, dependencies, migrations, endpoints or character assets have been wired into the app, and this documentation pass makes no commit, push or deployment claim.
- Scheduling estimates should follow the first asset/rendering spike and a representative page slice. The sequencing and acceptance gates are defined now; a calendar promise would hide the current 3D/native uncertainty.
