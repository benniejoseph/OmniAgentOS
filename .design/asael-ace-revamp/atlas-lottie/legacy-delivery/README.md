# Historical ATLAS deliveries

These retired raster posters/sprites and earlier standalone Lottie compositions
were moved out of `public/` on 7 October 2026. They are retained as historical
artifacts and are not shipped or requested by the current app.

The current vectors are authored in `../source/generate.mjs` and delivered from
`public/companion/atlas-lottie/`. Historical measurement scripts and fixture
contracts that refer to the original public paths are retired references, not
current rendering validation. Do not republish this directory.
